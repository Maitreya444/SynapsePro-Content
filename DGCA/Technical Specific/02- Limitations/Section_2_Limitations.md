# c172r-ch2-vne

Question
What is the Never Exceed Speed (VNE) for the Cessna 172R, expressed in both indicated and calibrated airspeeds?

Answer
The Never Exceed Speed (VNE) is 163 KIAS or 160 KCAS.

Explanation
VNE is the maximum speed permitted for the aircraft. It is identified by the red line on the airspeed indicator and must not be exceeded. This is a critical operating limitation that the pilot must observe whenever managing airspeed, particularly during high-speed flight.

# c172r-ch2-vno

Question
What is the Maximum Structural Cruising Speed (VNO) for the Cessna 172R, and under what atmospheric conditions may it be exceeded?

Answer
The Maximum Structural Cruising Speed (VNO) is 129 KIAS or 126 KCAS. It may be exceeded only in smooth air and with extreme caution.

Explanation
VNO marks the upper boundary of the normal operating range and the beginning of the caution range on the airspeed indicator. The pilot must observe the published atmospheric condition when operating above VNO.

# c172r-ch2-vfe

Question
What are the maximum flap extended speeds (VFE) for the Cessna 172R based on flap deflection?

Answer
The maximum speed is 110 KIAS for 10° of flaps and 85 KIAS for flap settings from 10° to 30°.

Explanation
The Cessna 172R has different maximum permissible speeds depending on the amount of flap extension. The 10° setting has a limit of 110 KIAS, while flap settings from 10° through 30° have a limit of 85 KIAS.

The white arc identifies the airspeed range associated with operation with wing flaps extended. Its lower boundary is 33 KIAS and its upper boundary is 85 KIAS.

# c172r-ch2-single-tank

Question
Under what flight condition is operation on a single fuel tank (LEFT or RIGHT) permitted?

Answer
Operation on either the LEFT or RIGHT fuel tank is restricted to level flight only. With 1/4 tank or less, prolonged uncoordinated flight such as slips or skids is prohibited while operating on a single tank.

Explanation
The POH places restrictions on operation using only one fuel tank. The LEFT or RIGHT selector position is limited to level flight, and additional restrictions apply when the selected tank contains 1/4 tank or less.
