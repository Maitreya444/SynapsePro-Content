# Card G055: ~ことができます

Question
What does 「〜ことができます」mean?

Answer
To be able to do something / can do (expressing ability or potential).

Marathi
मला जपानी बोलता येते — I can speak Japanese. Japanese dictionary form + ことができます and Marathi verb + ता येते share a beautiful parallel where the action is nominalized before expressing ability.

Example
Easy: 私は漢字を読むことができます (わたしは かんじを よむ ことができます) — I can read kanji. (Why: The dictionary form 読む is nominalized by ことができます to show reading ability.)
Medium: ここでカードで払うことができます (ここで カードで はらう ことができます) — You can pay by card here. (Why: Expresses situational possibility of payment using the verb 払う.)
Hard: 私は日本語で簡単な手紙を書くことができます (わたしは にほんごで かんたんな てがみを かく ことができます) — I can write a simple letter in Japanese. (Why: Combines multiple modifiers like "in Japanese" and "simple letter" with the dictionary form verb "書く" to express capability.)

Mnemonic
こと turns a verb into "the act of [verb]-ing" (like English "-ing" as a noun), and できます means
"is possible" — so literally "the act of doing X is possible." Picture turning the verb into a
noun-shaped object before handing it to できます.

AI Notes
Common mistake: attaching ことができます to the ます-form instead of the plain dictionary form (❌
食べますことができます, ✅ 食べることができます). Also don't confuse this with the shorter potential-verb
form (食べられます) — N5 typically expects ことができます first; the direct potential form is more N4.
