# G047: AよりBのほうが

Question
What does 「AよりBのほうが」mean?

Answer
B is more [adjective] than A.

Marathi
A पेक्षा B जास्त (विशेषण) आहे — B is more [adjective] than A. (The postposition "पेक्षा" (peksha) directly mirrors the comparison particle "より", and "B च्या बाजूने" or simply "B" matches "B のほうが" to indicate the subject possessing the higher degree of the quality).

Example
Easy: 猫より犬のほうが好きです。(ねこ より いぬ のほうが すき です。) — I like dogs more than cats. (Why: Shows a simple comparative preference between two common nouns.)
Medium: カタカナより漢字のほうが難しいです。(かたかな より かんじ のほうが むずかしい です。) — Kanji is more difficult than katakana. (Why: Compares two scripts using a standard い-adjective.)
Hard: 自転車より電車のほうが速くて便利です。(じてんしゃ より でんしゃ のほうが はやくて べんり です。) — Trains are faster and more convenient than bicycles. (Why: Compares two modes of transportation using a compound adjective description linked via て-form.)

Mnemonic
より sounds like "more-y" — read it as "more than." のほうが = "the side/direction of" — literally
"more than A, B's side [wins]."

AI Notes
Common mistake: dropping のほうが and just saying "AとBが" — that changes the sentence into a plain
listing, not a comparison. The winning item always sits right before のほうが, not after より.

# G048: AとBとどちらが

Question
What does 「AとBとどちらが」mean?

Answer
Which is more [adjective], A or B?

Marathi
A आणि B मध्ये कोणते जास्त (विशेषण) आहे? — Which is more [adjective], A or B? (The double "と" particle marks both options like "आणि", while "どちらが" functions exactly as the interrogative "कोणते/कोणता" in Marathi to ask for a choice between two).

Example
Easy: 犬と猫とどちらが好きですか。(いぬ と ねこ と どちら が すき です か。) — Which do you like more, dogs or cats? (Why: Standard polite question pattern comparing preferences for two animal nouns.)
Medium: 日本語と英語とどちらが難しいですか。(にほんご と えいご と どちら が むずかしい です か。) — Which is more difficult, Japanese or English? (Why: Compares the difficulty of two languages using an い-adjective with the question particle か.)
Hard: 今日と明日とどちらが暇ですか。(きょう と あした と どちら が ひま です か。) — Which day are you more free, today or tomorrow? (Why: Interrogates comparative free time using temporal nouns and a な-adjective.)

Mnemonic
どちら = "which of the two" — picture holding up two hands, one for A and one for B, and asking
"dochira?" (this one, or that one?).

AI Notes
Common mistake: using どれ (which of three or more) instead of どちら — どちら is required specifically
when comparing exactly two named options. Also don't drop the second と — both A and B need their
own と (A と B と どちらが).

# G049: ~の中でいちばん

Question
What does 「~の中でいちばん」mean?

Answer
The most [adjective] among/in [category] (superlative).

Marathi
~ मध्ये सर्वात... — The most [adjective] in/among ~. (The compound postposition "の中で" directly corresponds to the Marathi locative group marker "मध्ये", and "いちばん" matches the superlative modifier "सर्वात" or "सगळ्यात").

Example
Easy: 果物の中でりんごがいちばん好きです。(くだもの の なか で りんご が いちばん すき です。) — Apples are my favorite among fruits. (Why: Identifies a single favorite item from a clear category noun.)
Medium: 家族の中でだれがいちばん背が高いですか。(かぞく の なか で だれ が いちばん せ が たかい です か。) — Who is the tallest person in your family? (Why: Formulates a superlative question within a social group noun using the interrogative "だれ".)
Hard: 一年の中で八月がいちばん暑いです。(いちねん の なか で はちがつ が いちばん あつい です。) — August is the hottest month of the year. (Why: States a superlative climate fact within a temporal cycle category.)

Mnemonic
の中で = "inside of" (中 = inside), いちばん = "number one" (一 = one, 番 = rank/number) — literally
"inside [category], (ranked) number one."

AI Notes
Common mistake: reaching for もっと ("more") when a superlative is needed — もっと only works for
comparatives, never for "the most." Also の中で always needs a group/category noun before it (fruits,
family, the year); it can't stand alone as "the most."
