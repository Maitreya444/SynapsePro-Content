# Card 36: G036
Question
What does 「い-adjective (affirmative)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a present or future affirmative state or quality using an adjective ending in "い" in its polite form.

Marathi
हा चहा खूप गरम आहे। — This tea is very hot. Both languages share the SOV sentence structure where the adjective precedes the closing polite state marker (です / आहे).

Example
Easy: 今日は寒いです。(きょうはさむいです) — Today is cold. (Why: The "い" adjective 寒い ends in "いです" for polite present affirmation.)
Medium: この本はとても面白いです。(このほんはとてもおもしろいです) — This book is very interesting. (Why: The adverb とても modifies the "い" adjective to add emphasis.)
Hard: わたしの部屋は狭いですが、きれいです。(わたしのへやはせまいですが、きれいです) — My room is small, but it is clean. (Why: Links an "い" adjective clause using が to show contrast before a "な" adjective predicate.)

Mnemonic
い-adjectives already end in い — no extra copula needed to "make" them an adjective, just add です
for politeness (寒い → 寒いです).

AI Notes
Common mistake: adding な after an い-adjective when modifying a noun (❌ 寒いな日) — な is only for
な-adjectives; い-adjectives attach directly to the noun (寒い日).

# Card 37: G037
Question
What does 「い-adjective negative (~くないです)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a present or future negative state or quality for an "い" adjective by dropping "い" and adding "くないです".

Marathi
हा रस्ता चांगला नाही। — This road is not good. Both languages show a parallel where the affirmative adjective is modified at its end to express negation before the final verb/copula.

Example
Easy: 今日は寒くないです。(きょうはさむくないです) — Today is not cold. (Why: The "い" of 寒い is dropped and replaced with くないです to conjugate to negative.)
Medium: その辞書はあまり良くないです。(そのじしょはあまりよくないです) — That dictionary is not very good. (Why: The irregular adjective いい changes to よ before adding くないです, modified by the negative-favouring adverb あまり.)
Hard: このアパートの家賃は安くないですが、便利です。(このあぱーとのやちんはやすくないですが、べんりです) — The rent of this apartment is not cheap, but it is convenient. (Why: Shows a negative "い" adjective contrasting clause linked to a positive "な" adjective.)

Mnemonic
Drop the final い, add くない — "i" leaves, "ku-nai" arrives.

AI Notes
Common mistake: forgetting いい (good) is irregular — its negative is よくない, not いくない. Memorize
いい/よい as the one standout exception among い-adjectives.

# Card 38: G038
Question
What does 「い-adjective past (~かったです)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a past affirmative state or quality for an "い" adjective by dropping "い" and adding "かったです".

Marathi
काल खूप थंडी होती। — Yesterday it was very cold. Both systems place the past-inflected descriptive state right at the end of the sentence.

Example
Easy: 昨日のお祭りは楽しかったです。(きのうのおまつりはたのしかったです) — Yesterday's festival was fun. (Why: The adjective 楽しい drops "い" and takes かったです to indicate the past state.)
Medium: 先週のテストはとても難しかったです。(せんしゅうのてすとはとてもむずかしかったです) — Last week's test was very difficult. (Why: Inflects the long N5 adjective 難しい into its polite past affirmative form.)
Hard: 昨日は天気が良かったですから、海へ行きました。(きのうはてんきがよかったですから、うみへいきました) — Yesterday the weather was good, so I went to the sea. (Why: Uses the past form of いい (よかった) followed by から to express a reason.)

Mnemonic
Drop い, add かった — "i" becomes "katta" (sounds like something that already "got-ta" happen).

AI Notes
Common mistake: forgetting いい's irregular stem again — the past of いい is よかった, not いかった.
Also don't add でした after かったです; the past tense is already built into かった.

# Card 39: G039
Question
What does 「な-adjective (affirmative)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a present or future affirmative state or quality using a "な" adjective paired with the polite copula "です".

Marathi
हा मुलगा हुशार आहे। — This boy is smart. Adjectives in both systems can pair directly with a copula at the end without internal inflection changes in basic descriptive statements.

Example
Easy: この公園は静かです。(このこうえんはしずかです) — This park is quiet. (Why: The "な" adjective 静か pairs directly with the polite copula です to state a present affirmative condition.)
Medium: あの留学生は日本語がとても上手です。(あのりゅうがくせいはにほんごがとてもじょうずです) — That exchange student is very good at Japanese. (Why: The "な" adjective 上手 is used with the particle が to state a point of proficiency.)
Hard: この町はにぎやかですから、買い物が便利です。(このまちはにぎやかですから、かいものがべんりです) — This town is lively, so shopping is convenient. (Why: Connects two affirmative "な" adjective statements using the causal particle から.)

Mnemonic
な-adjectives act just like nouns — no ending change needed, just tack on です as-is (静か + です).

AI Notes
Common mistake: assuming な-adjectives need くない/かった like い-adjectives — they don't. Treat them
exactly like nouns for every conjugation (です / ではありません / でした).

# Card 40: G040
Question
What does 「な-adjective negative (~れません)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a present or future negative state or quality for a "な" adjective using "では（じゃ）ありません".

Marathi
हा परिसर शांत नाही। — This area is not quiet. Both structures end the clause with a negative copula/marker (ではありません / नाही) following the noun-like adjective.

Example
Easy: 今日は暇ではありません。(きょうはひまではありません) — I am not free today. (Why: The "な" adjective 暇 is negated politely using ありません.)
Medium: 私はあの人があまり好きじゃありません。(わたしはあのひとがあまりすきじゃありません) — I do not like that person very much. (Why: Uses the conversational negative じゃありません with the descriptive "な" adjective 好き.)

Mnemonic
Same negation as nouns: な-adjective + ではありません (or じゃありません) — no special adjective-only
rule to learn.

AI Notes
Common mistake: applying くない to a な-adjective (❌ 静かくない) — that's the い-adjective rule.

# Card 41: G041
Question
What does 「な-adjective past (~でした)」mean?

Answer
Expresses a past affirmative state or quality for a "な" adjective using the past polite copula "でした".

Marathi
तो माणूस प्रसिद्ध होता। — That man was famous. The past copula was (でした / होता) sits at the very end of the sentence in both SOV systems.

Example
Easy: 昨日は暇でした。(きのうはひまでした) — I was free yesterday. (Why: The "な" adjective 暇 is placed in the polite past tense using でした.)
Medium: 子どものとき、勉強が嫌いでした。(こどものとき、べんきょうがきらいでした) — When I was a child, I hated studying. (Why: Combines a time clause with the past state of a "な" adjective.)
Hard: 昨日のテストは簡単でしたから、時間が余りました。(きのうのてすとはかんたんでしたから、じかんがあまりました) — Yesterday's test was easy, so I had time left over. (Why: Explains a past outcome using a past "な" adjective cause linked by から.)

Mnemonic
な-adjective + でした — again, identical to how a plain noun takes its past tense.

AI Notes
Common mistake: writing 静かでしたです (a doubled copula) — でした alone is already the complete
past-polite form, no extra です needed after it.

# Card 42: G042
Question
What does 「Adjective + の (nominalization)」mean?

Answer
Turns an adjective into a noun pronoun meaning "the [adjective] one" to avoid repeating the noun itself.

Marathi
मला लाल वाला चेंडू दे, काळा वाला नको। — Give me the red ball, not the black one. The suffix "-वाला" (-wala) in the translation performs the exact same nominalizing function as the Japanese "の".

Example
Easy: 青いのが欲しいです。(あおいのがほしいです) — I want the blue one. (Why: Nominalizes the "い" adjective 青い to represent an object, avoiding repetition.)
Medium: 賑やかなのより静かなのが好きです。(にぎやかなのよりしずかなのがすきです) — I prefer a quiet one to a lively one. (Why: Nominalizes two "な" adjectives by appending なの, making a comparison.)
Hard: 新しいペンがありますか。古いのはもう書けません。(あたらしいぺんがありますか。ふるいのはもうかけません) — Do you have a new pen? The old one can no longer write. (Why: Uses の to replace the noun "pen" in the second sentence to maintain natural flow.)

Mnemonic
の here stands in for "one" — the missing noun swapped out for a placeholder, like English "the red
one."

AI Notes
Common mistake: forgetting な-adjectives keep their な before の (静かな + の = 静かなの), while
い-adjectives attach の directly with no extra particle (青い + の = 青いの).

# Card 43: G043
Question
What does 「~くて / ~で (connecting adjectives)」mean?

Answer
Connects two or more adjectives in a single sentence, meaning "and" (using "~くて" for "い" adjectives and "~で" for "な" adjectives).

Marathi
हा रस्ता रुंद आणि चांगला आहे। — This road is wide and good. Both languages connect multiple descriptors sequentially before ending the sentence with a single final verb/copula.

Example
Easy: この部屋は広くて明るいです。(このへやはひろくてあかるいです) — This room is spacious and bright. (Why: Connects the "い" adjective 広い by changing its ending to くて, followed by the second adjective.)
Medium: あの図書館は静かでとてもいいです。(あのとしょかんはしずかでとてもいいです) — That library is quiet and very good. (Why: Connects the "な" adjective 静か by adding で, followed by the "い" adjective sentence-ender.)
Hard: 日本の地下鉄はきれいで便利ですが、少し高いです。(にほんのちかてつはきれいでべんりですが、すこしたかいです) — Japan's subways are clean and convenient, but a bit expensive. (Why: Connects two "な" adjectives with で, and then links to a contrasting "い" adjective clause using が.)

Mnemonic
い→くて, な→で — a simple swap-in ending: drop い and add くて, or just add で to a な-adjective.

AI Notes
Common mistake: using て after a な-adjective (❌ 静かて) instead of で — な-adjectives (and nouns)
both connect with で; only い-adjectives use くて.
